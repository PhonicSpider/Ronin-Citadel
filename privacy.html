3. Cookies and similar technologies

Today we set one cookie, only after you sign in, and it is strictly necessary to keep you signed in. Details are in the Cookie Policy. You can see and change your choices at any time with in the footer of every page.

4. Analytics and advertising

We do not use third-party analytics tools or advertising yet, and nothing on the site tracks you across other websites. We do count, from our own records, how many people were active each day and produce combined statistics and reports (for example growth, sign-ins and age ranges). Dashboards and nearly all reports hold only combined numbers; one report lists organizations with the username of their owner and needs a second staff member's approval before anyone can open it. We may add third-party analytics or advertising later. If we do:

  • we will update this policy first, naming each provider, what it collects, why, and for how long;
  • cookies and similar technologies for analytics or advertising will stay off until you agree to them, and you can withdraw that agreement as easily as you gave it;
  • we will respect the Global Privacy Control signal your browser may send, and treat it as a request not to sell or share your personal information for advertising;
  • if advertising counts as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under the law where you live, we will add a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link and honour it;
  • advertising partners may act as independent controllers of the data they collect. They will have their own privacy policies, which we will link to.

The age range statistics described in section 2 are anonymous and combined, and are not used to target ads at you.

5. Who at Ronin Citadel can see your data

A small number of authorised staff can look up accounts and organizations (members, machines, servers, invitations and activity) to give support, keep the service secure, and investigate abuse. Staff access to your data is read-only, requires two-factor authentication, and every time staff open an organization or account the view is written to the activity log. Staff cannot see your password, your two-factor secret or your date of birth.

6. Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers who process data for us under their own terms. Any analytics or advertising provider we add will be listed here before it starts:

  • Railway: hosts the portal and its database.
  • Cloudflare: network protection and delivery in front of the portal, and storage for game server files (which contain no personal data).
  • An email delivery provider: sends invitation and password-reset emails.

People in the same organization as you can see your username and role. Organization administrators can see the email addresses of people they invite. We may disclose data if the law or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect our users and service from harm.

7. Transfers outside your country

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on an adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses and similar safeguards.

9. How we protect it

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes. Two-factor secrets are encrypted at rest. Sessions can be ended from your settings and are checked on the server on every request. Access to organization data is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access and portability: Settings → Your data → Download my data gives you a copy, including your date of birth, as a JSON file.
  • Deletion: Settings → Your data → Delete my account. If you own an organization, delete it first.
  • Correction, restriction and objection: email us and we will help.
  • Complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office; in the EU, the authority of your country.

If you are in California or a similar US state, you also have the right to know what we collect, to have it deleted or corrected, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We respond within one month (or the shorter time your law requires).

11. Age limit

The portal is not for anyone under 16. We ask for your date of birth when you register and refuse sign-ups under that age without keeping any of the details entered. A date of birth cannot be checked against an identity document, so if you believe a child has registered, email us and we will delete the account.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the version above and tell signed-in users before it takes effect where the law requires.
