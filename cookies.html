Cookie policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Ronin Citadel uses a single cookie, and only after you sign in. It keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or any tracker that follows you to other sites.

Cookies we set

NameWhat it is forHow longType
__Secure-rc_session Proves that your browser owns your signed-in session so the site can renew your access without asking for your password again. It cannot be read by page scripts (HttpOnly), is only sent to this site (SameSite=Strict), and only over HTTPS. It is deleted when you sign out. Renewed each time you use the site. Ends after 7 days without use, and at most 30 days after you signed in. Strictly necessary

Cookies that are strictly necessary to provide something you asked for, such as staying signed in, do not need your consent, so we do not ask you to agree to it.

Browser storage (not cookies)

NameWhat it is forHow long
rc_user (sessionStorage) Your username and email, so pages can show your name without asking the server again. Contains no password or token. Until you close the tab or sign out
rc_consent (localStorage) Remembers your cookie choices so the notice does not appear on every page. 12 months, or until you clear site data

Your sign-in token for the current page is kept only in the page's memory and is gone when you leave the page.

Other companies

Every script, style and image on this site is served from the site itself. Your browser does not contact any other company's server to load a page. The site does sit behind Cloudflare, which protects it from attacks and may set its own security cookie (for example __cf_bm) to tell real visitors from bots. That cookie is strictly necessary for the same reason. See the Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data.

Controlling cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookie you will not be able to stay signed in. You can see every browser and device signed in to your account, and sign any of them out, under Settings → Where you're signed in.

Optional cookies: analytics and advertising

CategoryStatus todayWhat it would be for
AnalyticsNot in useAnonymous statistics about how the portal is used, to improve it.
AdvertisingNot in useShowing and measuring ads, which may involve cookies set by advertising partners.

You can say yes or no to each category now, using the notice on first visit or in the footer. Your choice is stored in your browser for 12 months and then we ask again. Nothing optional runs unless you said yes, so today your choice has no effect, but it will apply from the first day we add either category. You can change your mind at any time.

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, advertising stays off and cannot be switched on here.

Before we add any optional cookie we will update this page and the Privacy Policy to name the provider, what it collects and for how long.
